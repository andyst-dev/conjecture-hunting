   - The gadget–path–gadget family is settled for all n with gadgets of ≤ 9 vertices: only C_4 ends are optimal, and triangle ends always lose.
   - A weak annealing screen up to n = 30 found nothing.
   - Side finding: **n = 8 has 9 extremal graphs**, all 4+4 bipartite, of which the paper names 3. Verified by `verify_n8_extremal.py`.

Reproduction: 77 values agree, with exact hitting times from python-flint. Holds for all trees with n ≤ 21. The margin grows with n. A(T) is the total transmission irregularity.
