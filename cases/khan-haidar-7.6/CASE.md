# Khan–Haidar, Conjecture 7.6 (arXiv:2611.21670 v1)

**Status:** no counterexample in the classes searched (listed below)
**Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Tools:** Python, numpy, networkx, python-flint · **AI-assisted (Claude), unrefereed**

## Statement
Source: M. ul I. Khan and G. Haidar, *Resistance-regular graphs need not be regular*, arXiv:2611.21670v1.

- G is a connected simple graph with Laplacian L and Moore–Penrose pseudo-inverse L⁺.
- The effective resistance is r_uv = L⁺_uu + L⁺_vv − 2L⁺_uv.
- G is **resistance-regular** (RR) if Σ_w r_vw does not depend on v. Equivalently, diag(L⁺) is constant (= c).
- **Conjecture 7.6.** If G is RR and d_u < d_v, then S₂(u) < S₂(v), where S₂(v) = Σ_w r_vw². Equivalently, (L⁺²)_uu < (L⁺²)_vv.
- Identity used throughout (RR case): S₂(v) = 4n·c² + 4·(L⁺²)_vv.

## Prior status (from the paper)
- Proved when L has m = 3 distinct nonzero eigenvalues.
- Proved for m = 4 under the condition d_u + d_v + 1 < σ₁ (Prop. 7.7(b)), via σ₄((L⁺²)_vv − (L⁺²)_uu) = (d_v − d_u)(σ₁ − 1 − d_u − d_v).
- Proved for F(p), for C₆(t,t²) and for the blow-ups of Prop. 5.5.
- Checked on 138 catalogue graphs and 3654 members of the family G(s,a,k,h).
- Fails for weighted graphs (Example 7.8, a house graph).

## Findings
1. Two non-regular RR graphs not among the examples we could read in the paper. They still need to be checked against the ancillary catalogue. Verify with `verify_new_rr_graphs.py`, which is standalone and exact.
   - C₆[PM₄, CP₈, CP₈, PM₄, CP₈, CP₈]: n = 40, 356 edges, degrees 17 and 18, L⁺_vv = 6701/115200. S₂ = 1537199/2764800 (degree 17) < 1548539/2764800 (degree 18).
   - C₆[CP₂₀, K₃₆, K₃₆, CP₂₀, K₃₆, K₃₆]: n = 184, 8352 edges, degrees 90 and 91, L⁺_vv = 304487/27423360.
   - Conjecture 7.6 holds on both.
2. Across every RR graph found, the smallest relative margin (q_high − q_low)/q_high is 6.3% (on the 184-vertex graph).
3. **Case m = 4.** The open part is to show σ₁ > d_u + d_v + 1. `feas2.py` shows this does **not** follow from local spectral constraints alone. Those constraints are: nonnegative spectral weights p_w(k), the (L³)_ww identity with d_w ≤ N_w ≤ d_w·d_max, μ_max ≥ d_max + 1, μ₁ ≤ d_min, and multiplicities ≥ 1. Their continuous relaxation admits σ₁ − 1 − a − b < 0. A proof needs more structure.

## Open leads
- **m = 4, targeted search.** The relaxation's violating points have a tiny μ₁, two large eigenvalues and very unbalanced degree classes. Look for RR graphs with exactly 4 distinct nonzero Laplacian eigenvalues of that shape.
- **Tri-circulants of order ≥ 8.** Solve the two RR equations directly, for example by fixing the diagonal connection sets and enumerating off-diagonal ones with pruning, instead of brute-force enumeration.
- **Minimum order** (the paper places it between 13 and 16). The tri-circulants over Z₅ (15 vertices) are now excluded; the other classes remain open.
